How a commission starts

  1. Send a brief: what you need, where it will be used, references, deadline and budget.
  2. I reply with the scope, timing and price.
  3. We agree, and I start with a work-in-progress preview.
  4. You receive the final files in the formats we agreed.

Pricing depends on the scope. Send your brief and I will quote it.
